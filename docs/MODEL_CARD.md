# Model Card

- **Task:** estimate product demand and explore candidate price points.
- **Model:** Random Forest regression with categorical encoding and numeric scaling.
- **Training data:** synthetic observations generated by `src/data.py`.
- **Recommendation objective:** maximize predicted revenue within user-specified price bounds.
- **Profit proxy:** optional 30% revenue contribution proxy; not based on actual product costs.
- **Limitations:** observational synthetic relationships, no causal price experiment, competitor feeds, inventory constraints, cross-product substitution, customer segmentation, taxes or regulatory checks.
- **Use:** learning and portfolio demonstration only; review recommendations before any business use.
